02 — FEATURED PROJECT

The System Maker.

Development of an integrated Electrocoagulation–Adsorption–Membrane system for Palm Oil Mill Effluent (POME) treatment.

Environmental Technology

Integrated POME Treatment System

My main project combines three complementary treatment stages to progressively improve wastewater quality and explore future water-reuse potential.

POME→Electrocoagulation→Activated Carbon→Membrane→Treated Water

Electrocoagulation

Initial treatment stage designed to destabilize and remove pollutants through electrochemical reactions and floc formation.

Adsorption

Activated carbon is used as a polishing stage to adsorb remaining dissolved organic compounds and contaminants.

Membrane Filtration

A final separation and polishing stage intended to further improve treated-water quality.
